Method

Four steps, all reproducible.

One seeded generator feeds everything. The same seed gives the same bytes, in Node and in this page.

Generate

Warehouses, zones, locations, SKUs and order lines with Zipf demand, peak windows and oversized, cold and hazard goods.

Slot

Baseline is a random compatible spot. Optimized gives the hottest SKU the nearest spot that fits and is allowed.

Measure

The same demand is replayed on both layouts: walking distance, throughput and replenishment labor.

Test

Five edge cases are injected with labels. A detector that never sees the labels must find exactly those.
