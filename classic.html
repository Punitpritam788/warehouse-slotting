Warehouse Slotting Lab

Put fast-moving SKUs close to the dock, keep hazard, cold and oversized goods where they belong, and measure what it saves. All data is synthetic and generated live in your browser from a fixed seed.

Compatibility rules

A SKU needs the right zone and must physically fit (length, width, height, weight; no rotation).

Validation checks

Injected edge cases vs detector

The detector reads only the input tables. Ground truth is a separate file it never sees.

Formulas: pick distance = 2 x dock distance; replenishment minutes = 5 + 0.1 x quantity + 0.05 x dock metres; refill at 25% of slot capacity; labor capacity = 115% of a warehouse's busiest day. Throughput assumes 1.2 m/s walking and 30 s handling per line.
